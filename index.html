A MINUTE TO LEARN

Find your angle.

  1. Connect two neighbors. Take turns drawing one line between adjacent dots: horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  2. Keep it clean. Existing lines can’t be repeated, and lines can’t cross.
  3. Close a triangle. Each newly completed triangle earns you 0.5 points, even when other players drew its other sides.
  4. Keep the momentum. Score and you play again. Otherwise, the turn passes to the next player.
  5. Watch the clock. With the optional timer on, your turn is skipped after 20 seconds.

Choose “Finish game” when you’re ready to count up. Highest score wins. Equal scores share the win.

On a phone: tap two dots, no dragging needed. Use Enlarge for more space, then scroll the board. Keyboard: arrows to move, Enter to select, Esc to clear.
